### POCSO Act, 2012 Section 11 & 12 — Sexual harassment of a child and punishment

**Section Number:** 11 & 12
**Section Name:** Sexual harassment of a child and punishment

**Description:**
Defines sexual harassment of a child (including showing pornography, repeated/persistent contact and online enticement) and prescribes punishment.

**Applicability:**
Online grooming, enticement and sexual harassment of minors in sextortion/CSAM cases.

**Evidence Requirements:**
Grooming/communication records, the offending content, victim age proof, and child-friendly statement under POCSO procedure.

**Related Crime Categories:** Sextortion, CSAM

**Investigator Notes:**
POCSO procedure (child-friendly recording, support persons, timelines) is mandatory; coordinate with the Child Welfare Committee.

**Common Investigation Mistakes:**
Using ordinary adult-victim procedure for a minor; delayed or non-child-friendly statement recording.

### POCSO Act, 2012 Section 13 & 14 — Use of a child for pornographic purposes and punishment

**Section Number:** 13 & 14
**Section Name:** Use of a child for pornographic purposes and punishment

**Description:**
Criminalises the use of a child in any form of media for sexual gratification (production of CSAM) and prescribes punishment.

**Applicability:**
Production/creation of CSAM and use of children in sexual content.

**Evidence Requirements:**
The CSAM (handled under strict controls), production/upload evidence, device forensics, and victim identification.

**Related Crime Categories:** Sextortion, CSAM

**Investigator Notes:**
Always charge together with IT Act S.67B. Apply mandatory reporting (S.19–22) and strict evidence-handling.

**Common Investigation Mistakes:**
Charging IT Act S.67B alone and omitting POCSO; mishandling CSAM evidence.

### POCSO Act, 2012 Section 15 — Punishment for storage/possession of child pornographic material

**Section Number:** 15
**Section Name:** Punishment for storage/possession of child pornographic material

**Description:**
Punishes storage or possession of CSAM with intent to share/transmit/display/distribute (or failure to delete/report), with graded penalties.

**Applicability:**
Possession, storage, transmission and distribution of CSAM — the SOP's CSAM/sextortion chapters.

**Evidence Requirements:**
Forensic proof of possession/storage on seized devices/cloud, hashes, and evidence of intent to transmit/distribute.

**Related Crime Categories:** Sextortion, CSAM

**Investigator Notes:**
Possession itself is an offence; intent to share aggravates it. Establish custody/control of the storage.

**Common Investigation Mistakes:**
Failing to forensically tie the storage to the accused's control; not establishing intent where the higher penalty is sought.

### POCSO Act, 2012 Section 19–22 — Mandatory reporting and procedural safeguards

**Section Number:** 19–22
**Section Name:** Mandatory reporting and procedural safeguards

**Description:**
Imposes mandatory reporting of CSAM/child sexual offences, child-friendly recording of statements and medical examination, and penalises failure to report.

**Applicability:**
Procedural backbone for every child-victim cyber case.

**Evidence Requirements:**
Reporting records, child-friendly statement, medical/forensic reports, and CWC coordination documentation.

**Related Crime Categories:** Sextortion, CSAM

**Investigator Notes:**
Failure to report is itself an offence; intermediaries and individuals have reporting duties. Maintain victim anonymity.

**Common Investigation Mistakes:**
Non-reporting or delayed reporting; breaching child victim's confidentiality/identity.
